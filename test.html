Helicopter crash outside Montreal kills all 4 occupants

By TORUL Tax Advisors  ·  Mississauga, Ontario  ·  September 2026

A helicopter crashed in a field near the Rang des Écossais off Highway 10 in the municipality of Sainte-Brigide-d'Iberville on Saturday afternoon, killing all four occupants on board. The Sûreté du Québec (SQ) major crimes unit has taken over the investigation.

What happened

SQ spokesperson Brigitte Dorsainville said the crash occurred at around 2:30 p.m. ET and the helicopter was on fire when first responders arrived. All four occupants were declared dead at the scene.

Police were unable to provide details on the ownership of the helicopter, where it took off, or the identities of the victims, citing the early stages of the investigation. The fire has since been brought under control.

Important: The SQ's major crimes unit has taken over the file, and the Transportation Safety Board of Canada (TSB) has deployed a team of investigators to the site.

Investigation underway

In a statement to CBC News, the TSB confirmed it has sent investigators to the crash site. Sainte-Brigide-d'Iberville is located roughly 55 kilometres southeast of Montreal.

The major crimes unit's involvement is standard procedure in fatal incidents, though authorities have not indicated any criminal suspicion at this time.

💡 Tip: If you or someone you know is affected by this tragedy, support resources are available through Quebec's mental health services and Victim Services.
